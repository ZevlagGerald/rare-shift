## What did you build?
RARE//SHIFT is a phase-shifting survival action roguelite where your ownership-verified Rare Friend's canonical animation becomes the signature weapon geometry and the battlefield law that decides which threats are corporeal.

The selected Friend is mechanically central rather than a cosmetic skin: canonical frame differences drive DELTA weapon geometry, while SHIFT flips the arena between Phase A and Phase B and rewrites target authority. The submission includes five distinct weapon families with qualified Rank I→V behavior and a deterministic four-slot build system.

> **Your Friend is not a skin. Its animation is the rules.**

## How does it use Rare Friends?
FriendSDK v0.1.2 handles wallet connection, Robinhood-network flow, hardwired Generations eligibility, ownership verification, Friend selection, and sandbox hosting. RARE//SHIFT uses the selected Friend's canonical animation data directly in combat geometry and phase mechanics.

## How do you play?
- WASD / arrow keys: move
- Space: SHIFT Phase A ↔ B
- touch controls: movement and SHIFT on supported narrow screens
- click/tap draft cards: choose upgrades
- weapons auto-fire

A-aligned threats are corporeal in A and ghosted in B; B-aligned threats reverse that relationship; COMMON threats remain dangerous in both. Survive, reposition, SHIFT, collect XP, and draft a four-weapon build.
